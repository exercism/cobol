       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. COUNT-WORD.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01 WS-TEXT     PIC X(40) VALUE "the cat and the hat".
       01 WS-WORD     PIC X(10).
       01 WS-POS      PIC 99 VALUE 1.
       01 WS-COUNT    PIC 9 VALUE 0.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           PERFORM UNTIL WS-POS > LENGTH OF WS-TEXT
               UNSTRING WS-TEXT DELIMITED BY ALL SPACE
                   INTO WS-WORD WITH POINTER WS-POS
               IF WS-WORD = "the"
                   ADD 1 TO WS-COUNT
               END-IF
           END-PERFORM
           DISPLAY '"the" appears ' WS-COUNT ' times'
           STOP RUN.
